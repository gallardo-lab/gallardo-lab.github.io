Dr. Hugo Gallardo-Blanco

Targeted Nanomedicine and Oncogenic Protein Inhibition Strategies

Universidad Autónoma de Nuevo León – School of Medicine

Research Programs

  • Targeted nanomedicine: receptor-directed platforms and biologically responsive biomaterials
  • Oncogenic inhibition: peptide blockers and degradation-inspired concepts for challenging targets
  • Mechanistic trafficking: uptake routes, intracellular routing, and functional endpoints
  • Translational genomics: population-focused molecular insights linked to clinical phenotypes

Current Projects

  • HER2-targeted aptamer nanosystems for selective cellular inhibition
  • Competitive blockade and destabilization of Myc-dependent transcriptional activity
  • Molecular response profiling after oncogenic pathway interference
  • Development of biologically guided delivery platforms for intracellular targets

Capabilities

  • Biomaterial/nanosystem design: formulation, functionalization concepts, and stability rationale
  • In vitro evaluation: cytotoxicity workflows, methodological controls, and result interpretation
  • Mechanistic assays: uptake/trafficking hypotheses with experiment-ready endpoints
  • Data-driven readouts: transcriptomic response framing and pathway-level interpretation

Note: exact assays and instrumentation can be detailed upon request depending on collaboration needs.
